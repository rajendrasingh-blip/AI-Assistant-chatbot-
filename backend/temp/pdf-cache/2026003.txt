In continuation of the discussions held in the meeting under the
Chairmanship of the Worthy Secretary, School Education, Punjab regarding the
challenges in operationalizing the provisions of Section 12(1) (C) of " The Right of
Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009" the matter has been
examined in detail. The arrangements have accordingly been approved to ensure
effective implementation of the aforesaid provisions. All concerned officers and
branches shall ensure strict compliance with the above directions. These
arrangements are issued in order to facilitate effective implementation of Section
12(1)(C) of " The Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act 2009" and
shall remain subject to further directions issued by the Government from time to
time.
